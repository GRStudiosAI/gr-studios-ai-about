## About GR Studios AI LLC

GR Studios AI LLC is an independent digital creation, technology, software development, and entertainment company focused on innovation, creativity, integrity, and technological advancement.

The company brings together multiple areas of digital development and creative production under one corporate identity.

Our mission is to develop meaningful digital experiences, innovative technologies, creative entertainment, and professional solutions while maintaining independence and integrity.

## Meaning Behind Our Name

GR Studios AI LLC stands for **Gotham Repacks Studios Above Integrity LLC**.

The company name incorporates three names associated with former business endeavors:

- Gotham Repacks
- Studios
- Above Integrity

These former business endeavors are no longer used, and the founder is no longer affiliated with them.

Their names were retained as a personal reminder of past business experiences, broken trust, setbacks, hardships, and valuable lessons learned.

They represent the determination to move forward independently, overcome challenges, and build something meaningful.

These historical names do not represent current partnerships, subsidiaries, affiliations, or business relationships.

### What Does AI Stand For?

The letters **AI** in GR Studios AI LLC stand for **Above Integrity**, not Artificial Intelligence.

Although artificial intelligence is among the technologies explored and utilized by the company, it is not the original meaning behind the company name.
