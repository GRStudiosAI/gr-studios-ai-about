## 1. Purpose

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes professional customer assistance, reliable technical support, responsible maintenance, and constructive community engagement as important components of its corporate operations.

This Corporate Support and Customer Assistance Policy establishes the procedures, responsibilities, service boundaries, communication standards, and support requirements associated with company-managed products, services, websites, applications, software, and digital platforms.

The objectives of this policy are to:

- Establish professional support procedures.
- Provide clear communication channels.
- Encourage accurate and responsible issue reporting.
- Protect customer privacy and confidential information.
- Establish appropriate technical support expectations.
- Distinguish community assistance from commercial support.
- Define maintenance and service responsibilities.
- Encourage timely investigation of reported problems.
- Establish appropriate escalation procedures.
- Support continuous product and service improvements.

## 11. Preparing a Technical Support Request

Before submitting a technical support request, users should review available documentation and existing reports where practical.

A complete support request should include:

- A descriptive title.
- The affected product or service.
- The affected version or release.
- Relevant operating system information.
- Relevant browser information.
- Relevant hardware information where applicable.
- A clear description of the problem.
- Expected behavior.
- Actual behavior.
- Relevant error messages.
- Steps necessary to reproduce the problem.
- Previously attempted troubleshooting steps.
- Sanitized diagnostic logs.
- Relevant screenshots or recordings.
- Information about recent configuration changes.

Accurate and complete information assists maintainers in identifying and investigating reported problems.

Passwords, access tokens, private keys, payment details, and confidential customer records must not be included.

## 12. Bug Reporting Requirements

Bug reports should describe identifiable problems affecting the intended operation of a product or service.

Where possible, reporters should:

- Confirm the affected version.
- Review existing bug reports.
- Determine whether the issue can be reproduced.
- Identify relevant environmental conditions.
- Provide clear reproduction steps.
- Include sanitized supporting evidence.
- Identify whether the problem affects previous versions.
- Describe any available workaround.
- Identify whether unofficial modifications are installed.

Reports should distinguish confirmed observations from assumptions or suspected causes.

Maintainers may request additional information before an investigation can proceed.

A support request may be closed when:

- The reported problem has been resolved.
- Appropriate troubleshooting guidance has been provided.
- A corrective update has been released.
- The request duplicates an existing report.
- The affected functionality is unsupported.
- Necessary information remains unavailable.
- The request has been redirected appropriately.
- The request falls outside applicable support requirements.
- The requested functionality is not planned.
- The issue cannot reasonably be reproduced.
- The customer confirms that assistance is no longer required.

Closure does not necessarily establish that every underlying concern has been permanently resolved.

Requests may be reopened or reconsidered when justified by additional information.
