## 1. Security Commitment

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes cybersecurity, privacy, confidentiality, data protection, and responsible technology development as essential components of its corporate operations.

This policy establishes the security principles, vulnerability reporting procedures, disclosure requirements, and security expectations associated with company-managed websites, applications, software, repositories, services, and digital infrastructure.

GR Studios AI LLC is committed to pursuing:

- Secure software development practices.
- Responsible vulnerability management.
- Protection of confidential information.
- Secure infrastructure and application architecture.
- Appropriate encryption and authentication standards.
- Responsible handling of security incidents.
- Continuous security improvements.
- Protection of customers, users, contributors, and business partners.

Security is an ongoing responsibility throughout the development, deployment, operation, and maintenance of company-managed technologies.

This policy does not automatically authorize security testing against any company-managed system.

## 4. Reporting Security Vulnerabilities

GR Studios AI LLC encourages responsible and confidential reporting of suspected security vulnerabilities.

Security vulnerabilities must not be reported through public GitHub Issues, public GitHub Discussions, social media comments, or other publicly accessible communication channels when the report contains sensitive technical information.

### Preferred Reporting Method

Use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting when enabled for the affected repository.

For this corporate website repository:

https://github.com/GRStudiosAI/gr-studios-ai-about/security

Select **Report a vulnerability** when the option is available.

For vulnerabilities affecting another official GR Studios AI LLC repository, use that repository's private vulnerability reporting channel or its documented security reporting procedure.

### Alternative Reporting Methods

If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, use a verified private security reporting channel published through the official corporate website or affected project.

Official corporate website:

https://grstudiosai.github.io/gr-studios-ai-about/

Official GitHub organization:

https://github.com/GRStudiosAI

If no verified private reporting channel is available, do not publicly disclose sensitive vulnerability information. Request an appropriate private reporting channel without including confidential technical details.

## 5. Vulnerability Report Requirements

A responsible vulnerability report should include sufficient information to allow authorized maintainers to investigate the reported issue safely.

Where applicable, include:

- A concise vulnerability title.
- The affected product, website, repository, or service.
- The affected software version.
- A description of the suspected vulnerability.
- The affected component or functionality.
- Relevant operating system and browser information.
- The potential security impact.
- Relevant timestamps and time zones.
- Sanitized screenshots or diagnostic information.
- Observed and expected behavior.
- Relevant error messages.
- Suggested remediation, if available.
- A preferred method of confidential communication.

Reports must not include unnecessary personal information, customer records, passwords, authentication tokens, private encryption keys, or other confidential credentials.

Any sensitive evidence must be handled through an authorized private reporting channel.

## 6. Responsible Security Research

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes the importance of legitimate security research and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.

However, security research must remain lawful, responsible, proportionate, and within explicitly authorized boundaries.

Public availability of a website, repository, application, or service does not constitute authorization to perform security testing.

Security researchers must:

- Obtain explicit authorization before testing live systems.
- Respect the privacy and confidentiality of users.
- Avoid accessing information belonging to other individuals.
- Avoid disrupting normal business operations.
- Avoid modifying or deleting company or customer information.
- Avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive information.
- Respect applicable laws and third-party service requirements.
- Report suspected vulnerabilities through approved private channels.
- Immediately discontinue activity that could cause unintended harm.

This policy does not establish unrestricted authorization, legal immunity, or a blanket security research safe harbor.

## 7. Prohibited Security Testing Activities

Unless separately and explicitly authorized in writing, the following activities are outside the permitted scope of security research:

- Denial-of-service and distributed denial-of-service testing.
- Unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
- Credential theft or unauthorized credential testing.
- Social engineering and impersonation.
- Phishing campaigns.
- Malware distribution.
- Unauthorized network scanning of company infrastructure.
- Unauthorized access to databases.
- Unauthorized collection of personal information.
- Modification or destruction of information.
- Physical security testing.
- Disruption of production services.
- Testing against customer accounts or customer-owned systems.
- Testing against third-party infrastructure without authorization.
- Unauthorized circumvention of security controls.
- Public disclosure of confidential vulnerability information.

Any authorization granted for security research must identify the applicable systems, boundaries, permitted activities, and authorization period.

## 8. Vulnerability Classification

Reported vulnerabilities may be classified according to their potential impact, affected systems, likelihood of exploitation, and other relevant circumstances.

| Severity | General Classification |
|---|---|
| Critical | Vulnerabilities presenting an immediate or exceptionally serious security risk |
| High | Vulnerabilities presenting substantial security, confidentiality, or operational risks |
| Medium | Vulnerabilities presenting meaningful but more limited security risks |
| Low | Vulnerabilities presenting minor or narrowly constrained security risks |
| Informational | Observations, recommendations, or security improvements without a demonstrated vulnerability |

Where appropriate, the Common Vulnerability Scoring System (CVSS) may be used to assist with vulnerability assessment.

Final severity classifications may differ from the initial assessment provided by the reporter.

## 9. Vulnerability Management Process

Reported security vulnerabilities may be handled through the following process:

1. Receipt of the confidential vulnerability report.
2. Initial review and acknowledgment, where possible.
3. Verification and validation.
4. Security impact assessment.
5. Severity classification.
6. Identification of affected systems.
7. Investigation and remediation planning.
8. Development of appropriate corrections.
9. Security validation and regression testing.
10. Deployment or distribution of corrective updates.
11. Coordinated disclosure, when appropriate.
12. Post-incident review and security improvements.

The complexity of the vulnerability, available resources, third-party dependencies, and operational requirements may affect investigation and remediation timelines.

## 10. Security Response Objectives

The following timeframes are proposed internal objectives rather than guaranteed service-level agreements.

| Severity | Initial Review Objective | Remediation Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Within 24–48 hours where feasible | Immediate prioritization |
| High | Within 3 business days where feasible | High priority |
| Medium | Within 7 business days where feasible | Scheduled remediation |
| Low | Within 14 business days where feasible | Routine maintenance |
| Informational | As resources permit | Evaluated for future improvements |

Actual response and remediation times may vary.

Separate contractual security obligations may apply to individual customer projects or managed services.

## 11. Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure

GR Studios AI LLC supports responsible and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.

Security researchers are requested to provide a reasonable opportunity for investigation, remediation, and deployment of security corrections before publicly disclosing sensitive vulnerability information.

Public disclosure should be coordinated to reduce unnecessary risks to customers, users, contributors, and affected infrastructure.

Disclosure arrangements may include:

- Confidential communication with the reporter.
- Agreement on an appropriate disclosure timeline.
- Preparation of security advisories.
- Distribution of corrective updates.
- Notification of affected parties where appropriate.
- Recognition of the reporting researcher with their consent.
- Coordination with affected third-party providers.

Nothing in this policy is intended to override applicable legal reporting obligations or lawful disclosure rights.

Official repositories should be maintained using appropriate repository security controls.

These may include:

- Restricted administrative permissions.
- Protected branches.
- Pull request review requirements.
- Dependency vulnerability alerts.
- Secret scanning.
- Push protection.
- Automated security checks.
- Controlled GitHub Actions permissions.
- Secure workflow configuration.
- Verified release procedures.
- Multi-factor authentication.

- Appropriate repository access management.

## 15. Authentication and Access Control

Company-managed systems should implement access controls appropriate to their security requirements.

These may include:

- Multi-factor authentication.
- Role-based access control.
- Least-privilege permissions.
- Secure account recovery procedures.
- Session management.
- Authentication monitoring.
- Administrative access restrictions.
- Secure credential storage.
- Account access reviews.
- Removal of obsolete access permissions.
- Appropriate account protection mechanisms.

Access to company-managed resources must be limited to authorized individuals and services.

Unauthorized access, credential sharing, impersonation, and misuse of administrative privileges are prohibited.

## 16. Encryption and Cryptographic Security

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes encryption as an important component of modern information security.

Where appropriate, company-developed systems may implement:

- Encryption of information in transit.
- Encryption of sensitive information at rest.
- Secure password hashing.
- Secure cryptographic key management.
- Digital signatures.
- Certificate-based authentication.
- Secure communication protocols.
- Encrypted backup storage.
- End-to-end encryption where supported and appropriate.
- Project-specific advanced encryption requirements.

Cryptographic implementations should use established, appropriately maintained technologies and libraries.

Encryption requirements, implementation details, key management responsibilities, and maintenance obligations may vary according to individual project agreements.

No encryption technology or implementation is represented as completely immune to compromise.

- Secure external links.

## 21. Security Incident Management

A security incident may include suspected or confirmed unauthorized access, credential exposure, information disclosure, infrastructure compromise, malicious activity, or other events affecting system confidentiality, integrity, or availability.

Where appropriate, incident management may include:

- Incident identification.
- Initial security assessment.
- Containment of affected systems.
- Protection of relevant evidence.
- Investigation of the incident.
- Credential revocation and replacement.
- Remediation of affected components.
- Restoration of affected services.
- Notification of affected parties where required.
- Coordination with relevant service providers.
- Documentation of findings.
- Post-incident security improvements.

Incident notifications will be evaluated according to applicable legal requirements, contractual obligations, and the circumstances of the incident.

Backup and recovery requirements should be established according to the operational needs of each system.

Appropriate measures may include:

- Regular backup procedures.
- Secure backup storage.
- Version-controlled source code.
- Infrastructure recovery documentation.
- Backup integrity verification.
- Controlled restoration procedures.
- Recovery testing.
- Protection against unauthorized backup access.
- Project-specific retention requirements.

## 23. Security Updates and Maintenance

Security updates may be distributed through official repositories, application distribution platforms, managed infrastructure, or other verified distribution channels.

Users are encouraged to:

- Install officially distributed security updates.
- Maintain supported software versions.
- Protect account credentials.
- Enable available account security features.
- Avoid unofficial or modified software distributions.
- Verify the authenticity of downloaded software.
- Review relevant security advisories.
- Report suspected security issues responsibly.

Security maintenance availability may vary according to product lifecycle status and applicable service agreements.

## 24. Security Research Recognition and Bug Bounties

GR Studios AI LLC may recognize individuals who responsibly report legitimate security vulnerabilities.

Recognition may include acknowledgment in security advisories, release notes, or other official communications, subject to the reporter's consent.

Unless an official bug bounty program is separately announced in writing:

- No monetary reward is guaranteed.
- No compensation agreement is automatically established.
- Submission of a vulnerability report does not create an entitlement to payment.
- Recognition is subject to review and approval.

Any future bug bounty program will be governed by its separately published eligibility requirements, scope, rules, and reward structure.

## 25. Public Discussions and Security Information

GitHub Discussions and GitHub Issues are intended for appropriate public communication, general support, feedback, announcements, and non-confidential technical discussions.

The following information must not be intentionally published through public community channels:

- Unresolved sensitive vulnerability details.
- Private security investigation information.
- Authentication credentials.
- API keys and access tokens.
- Private encryption keys.
- Customer records.
- Confidential business information.
- Private infrastructure configurations.
- Sensitive security incident evidence.

Community content may be moderated in accordance with the applicable Code of Conduct, Discussion Guidelines, and platform policies.

## 26. Security Limitations

No website, application, operating system, software product, network, encryption implementation, or digital infrastructure can be guaranteed to remain completely secure under every circumstance.

Security risks may arise from evolving threats, software defects, configuration errors, third-party dependencies, unauthorized modifications, or other circumstances.

GR Studios AI LLC aims to implement reasonable security practices appropriate to the systems and services involved.

This policy does not establish an absolute security guarantee, guaranteed uninterrupted availability, or a universal contractual service-level agreement.

Applicable statutory rights and separately agreed contractual obligations remain unaffected.
