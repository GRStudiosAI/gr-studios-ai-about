# Corporate and Community Code of Conduct

## GR Studios AI LLC

*Where Imagination Meets Intelligence.*

---

## 1. Purpose

GR Studios AI LLC is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, inclusive, secure, and productive environment across its official platforms, development projects, business operations, and managed communities.

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected from individuals participating in company-managed environments, including contributors, developers, community members, customers, moderators, administrators, and authorized representatives.

Our objective is to encourage constructive communication, responsible collaboration, technological innovation, creativity, and professional accountability.

Participation in company-managed communities is subject to this Code of Conduct, applicable platform policies, and applicable laws.

## 2. Corporate Values

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes the following principles as fundamental to its corporate and community identity:

- Integrity and accountability.
- Professionalism and mutual respect.
- Independent thinking and creativity.
- Responsible technological development.
- Honest and constructive communication.
- Fair and consistent treatment.
- Protection of privacy and confidentiality.
- Respect for intellectual property.
- Community safety and security.
- Continuous learning and improvement.
- Responsible collaboration.
- Transparency where appropriate.

These principles guide community management, development activities, professional interactions, and organizational decision-making.

## 3. Scope of Application

This Code of Conduct applies to participation within officially managed GR Studios AI LLC environments, including, where applicable:

- Official corporate websites.
- GitHub organizations and repositories.
- GitHub Discussions.
- GitHub Issues.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Software development communities.
- Official Discord servers.
- Company-managed communication platforms.
- Gaming communities.
- Application and operating system communities.
- Artificial intelligence platforms.
- Music and entertainment communities.
- Technical support channels.
- Official social media communities.
- Company-sponsored events.
- Authorized business communications.
- Other officially managed digital environments.

This policy may also apply when individuals are formally representing GR Studios AI LLC in external environments.

It does not grant the company unrestricted authority over unrelated private activities or independently operated communities.

Individual projects and platforms may establish additional requirements appropriate to their specific purpose.

## 4. Individuals Covered by This Policy

The standards established within this Code of Conduct apply to:

- Company owners and leadership.
- Authorized company representatives.
- Administrators and moderators.
- Employees and contractors, where applicable.
- Software developers.
- Project maintainers.
- Repository contributors.
- Community members.
- Customers and clients.
- Business collaborators.
- Volunteers.
- Visitors participating in managed communities.

Administrative authority, technical expertise, business relationships, or community status do not exempt an individual from appropriate standards of conduct.

## 5. Expected Standards of Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Treat others with dignity and fairness.
- Participate in constructive discussions.
- Respect differences in experience, knowledge, and opinions.
- Provide helpful and relevant feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism professionally.
- Acknowledge and correct mistakes when appropriate.
- Respect established community rules.
- Protect confidential and personal information.
- Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.
- Use official communication channels appropriately.
- Cooperate with legitimate moderation procedures.
- Avoid unnecessary disruption of community activities.
- Contribute to a productive and welcoming environment.
- Report serious misconduct through appropriate channels.

Participants are encouraged to resolve ordinary disagreements through respectful communication whenever reasonably possible.

## 6. Equality, Inclusion, and Non-Discrimination

GR Studios AI LLC aims to maintain an environment where individuals can participate without unlawful discrimination, harassment, intimidation, or targeted hostility.

Discriminatory conduct directed toward individuals or groups based on personal characteristics is prohibited.

Protected characteristics may include:

- Age.
- Race or ethnicity.
- National origin.
- Citizenship or immigration status.
- Religion or sincerely held beliefs.
- Disability.
- Medical condition.
- Sex.
- Gender identity or expression.
- Sexual orientation.
- Marital or family status.
- Military or veteran status.
- Other characteristics protected by applicable law.

Differences in technical experience, education, professional background, financial circumstances, or community status must not be used as justification for targeted harassment or humiliation.

Respectful disagreement and good-faith discussion remain permitted.

## 7. Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are prohibited within company-managed environments:

### Harassment and Abuse

- Targeted harassment.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Bullying or intimidation.
- Threatening behavior.
- Personal attacks.
- Deliberate humiliation.
- Stalking.
- Sexual harassment.
- Coordinated harassment campaigns.
- Encouraging others to harass an individual.

### Discrimination and Hate-Based Conduct

- Discriminatory harassment.
- Dehumanizing language directed at protected groups.
- Promotion of hatred or violence against individuals or communities.
- Targeted discriminatory insults.
- Advocacy of unlawful discrimination.

### Threats and Harmful Activities

- Credible threats of violence.
- Encouragement of violence against others.
- Extortion or blackmail.
- Exploitation or abuse.
- Promotion of unlawful harmful activities.
- Deliberate attempts to endanger community members.

### Fraud and Impersonation

- Impersonating company representatives.
- Falsely claiming administrative authority.
- Fraudulent business representations.
- Phishing or deceptive solicitations.
- Financial scams.
- Misrepresentation of official company partnerships.
- Unauthorized representation of GR Studios AI LLC.

### Community Disruption

- Spam and unsolicited mass advertising.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Coordinated attempts to obstruct legitimate discussions.
- Manipulation of community voting or reporting systems.
- Deliberate flooding of support channels.
- Circumvention of legitimate moderation restrictions.
- Malicious interference with community operations.

### Privacy and Confidentiality Violations

- Publishing private information without authorization.
- Doxxing.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential communications.
- Sharing customer records.
- Publishing authentication credentials.
- Unauthorized distribution of private project information.
- Deliberate exposure of confidential security reports.

## 8. Professional Communication Standards

Participants should communicate in a manner appropriate to the purpose of the platform.

Professional communication includes:

- Addressing the subject rather than attacking the individual.
- Providing clear and relevant explanations.
- Avoiding deliberately misleading statements.
- Distinguishing verified information from personal opinions.
- Respecting reasonable communication boundaries.
- Allowing others an opportunity to respond.
- Avoiding unnecessary escalation.
- Maintaining appropriate language in professional environments.

Criticism of software, company decisions, development practices, policies, products, or services is permitted when presented in good faith.

Disagreement alone does not constitute misconduct.

Negative reviews, legitimate complaints, and respectful criticism must not be treated as harassment merely because they are unfavorable.

## 9. GitHub Community Standards

Participants using official GR Studios AI LLC GitHub repositories must comply with this Code of Conduct and applicable GitHub policies.

### GitHub Discussions

GitHub Discussions may be used for:

- Official announcements.
- General community discussions.
- Questions and answers.
- Technical support.
- Suggestions and ideas.
- Product feedback.
- Development discussions.
- Community polls.
- Relevant business and technology discussions.

Participants should select the appropriate discussion category and avoid unnecessary duplicate posts.

### GitHub Issues

GitHub Issues should be used for relevant and actionable reports, including:

- Software defects.
- Website problems.
- Documentation corrections.
- Feature requests.
- Technical compatibility issues.
- Other project-related concerns.

Issues must not be used for harassment, personal disputes, confidential business negotiations, or public disclosure of sensitive security vulnerabilities.

### Pull Requests and Code Reviews

Participants are expected to:

- Follow repository contribution requirements.
- Provide accurate descriptions of proposed changes.
- Respect maintainers and reviewers.
- Respond constructively to review feedback.
- Avoid intentionally introducing harmful or unauthorized changes.
- Respect project licensing requirements.
- Avoid including confidential information or credentials.
- Respect legitimate project architecture and development decisions.

Maintainers retain discretion over whether proposed contributions are accepted, rejected, or returned for revision.

Contribution rejection does not automatically constitute unfair treatment.

## 10. Intellectual Property and Licensing

Participants must respect the intellectual property rights of GR Studios AI LLC and third parties.

Prohibited activities include:

- Unauthorized commercial exploitation of proprietary company materials.
- Fraudulent claims of ownership.
- Unauthorized use of company branding that creates confusion about affiliation.
- Knowingly submitting materials without the necessary rights or permissions.
- Removing required copyright or attribution notices.
- Distributing proprietary materials contrary to applicable licensing terms.
- Deliberate infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.

Public repository visibility does not automatically establish unrestricted permission to use proprietary materials.

Individual open-source projects may operate under separate licenses.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict activities expressly permitted by applicable law, valid licenses, or applicable platform terms.

Participants should review the applicable repository license, copyright documentation, and trademark policy.

## 11. Security and Responsible Technology Use

Participants must not misuse company-managed systems, development environments, repositories, applications, or infrastructure.

Prohibited activities include:

- Unauthorized access.
- Credential theft.
- Malware distribution.
- Unauthorized security testing.
- Deliberate introduction of malicious source code.
- Unauthorized collection of personal information.
- Attempts to disrupt production services.
- Unauthorized disclosure of sensitive vulnerabilities.
- Circumvention of legitimate access controls.
- Misuse of administrative permissions.
- Fraudulent distribution of modified company software.

Suspected security vulnerabilities must be reported through the procedures established in the official [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

Public Issues and Discussions must not be used to publish confidential vulnerability details.

Legitimate security research must remain within applicable authorization and legal boundaries.

## 12. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Content

GR Studios AI LLC recognizes artificial intelligence and automation as valuable technologies when used responsibly.

Participants using artificial intelligence tools, automated systems, or bots within company-managed environments must:

- Remain accountable for submitted content.
- Review generated contributions for accuracy and relevance.
- Respect intellectual property rights.
- Avoid generating deceptive or malicious content.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid automated harassment or spam.
- Follow project-specific automation requirements.
- Disclose automated contributions when required by project policy.

Artificial intelligence must not be used to impersonate individuals, fabricate official company statements, distribute harmful content, or misrepresent professional authorization.

AI-generated content is subject to the same community standards as other submitted content.

## 13. Protection of Minors and Community Safety

GR Studios AI LLC aims to maintain appropriate safety standards for communities that may include minors.

The following conduct is strictly prohibited:

- Sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
- Grooming or predatory behavior.
- Sexualized harassment directed toward minors.
- Requests for inappropriate personal images.
- Distribution of child sexual abuse material.
- Encouraging minors to participate in dangerous or unlawful activities.
- Attempts to manipulate minors into disclosing confidential personal information.
- Deliberate exposure of minors to prohibited adult content.

Suspected exploitation or immediate safety threats should be reported through appropriate platform safety mechanisms and relevant authorities when required.

Community administrators may restrict access to content or services according to applicable age requirements and platform policies.

## 14. Privacy and Confidential Information

Participants must respect the privacy of other individuals and organizations.

Confidential information may include:

- Residential addresses.
- Private telephone numbers.
- Personal email addresses.
- Government identification information.
- Financial information.
- Medical information.
- Authentication credentials.
- Customer records.
- Private business agreements.
- Unreleased proprietary source code.
- Internal security documentation.
- Private communications.
- Confidential project requirements.

Such information must not be published without appropriate authorization or another lawful basis.

Publicly accessible information must not be collected or redistributed for the purpose of harassment, intimidation, stalking, or exploitation.

## 15. Business and Customer Conduct

Individuals participating in business-related communications are expected to maintain appropriate professional standards.

This includes:

- Honest representation of project requirements.
- Respectful communication regarding pricing and services.
- Protection of confidential business information.
- Respect for agreed project boundaries.
- Compliance with applicable service agreements.
- Responsible handling of disagreements.
- Avoidance of fraudulent payment activity.
- Respect for intellectual property ownership.
- Appropriate use of official business channels.

Customers retain the right to raise legitimate complaints, dispute charges through lawful procedures, request clarification, and exercise applicable consumer rights.

Public community channels should not be used to disclose private customer information, confidential negotiations, or sensitive payment details.

Commercial disputes should be addressed through appropriate business communication channels and applicable agreements.

## 16. Advertising, Promotion, and Solicitation

Advertising and promotional activities must remain relevant to the purpose of the community and comply with applicable platform rules.

Unauthorized activities may include:

- Unsolicited mass advertising.
- Repeated promotional spam.
- Fraudulent investment solicitations.
- Deceptive affiliate promotions.
- Unauthorized commercial recruitment.
- Impersonation of official sponsors.
- Misrepresentation of business partnerships.
- Distribution of malicious promotional links.

Relevant project recommendations and legitimate community contributions are not automatically considered unauthorized advertising.

Commercial promotion may require prior approval from authorized community administrators.

## 17. Official Representation and Corporate Identity

Only authorized individuals may formally represent GR Studios AI LLC.

Participants must not falsely claim to be:

- Company owners.
- Company executives.
- Authorized employees.
- Official developers.
- Project maintainers.
- Community administrators.
- Business partners.
- Official sponsors.
- Authorized customer support representatives.

Historical names incorporated into the GR Studios AI LLC corporate identity do not establish current partnerships, subsidiaries, or affiliations with former business endeavors.

Unauthorized use of corporate branding to create misleading representations of affiliation is prohibited.

Independent opinions must not be falsely presented as official company statements.

## 18. Conflicts of Interest

Individuals exercising administrative, moderation, or decision-making authority should disclose relevant conflicts of interest when appropriate.

Conflicts may include:

- Personal disputes involving affected participants.
- Financial interests.
- Business relationships.
- Competitive interests.
- Close personal relationships.
- Other circumstances that could materially affect impartial judgment.

Where reasonably possible, moderation decisions involving significant conflicts of interest should be reviewed by another authorized individual.

Administrative authority must not be used to obtain improper personal advantages or retaliate against others.

## 19. Responsibilities of Administrators and Moderators

Authorized administrators and moderators are responsible for maintaining appropriate community standards.

Their responsibilities may include:

- Reviewing reported misconduct.
- Applying community rules consistently.
- Protecting confidential information.
- Removing prohibited content.
- Managing disruptive behavior.
- Documenting significant enforcement decisions.
- Protecting participants from targeted abuse.
- Reviewing moderation appeals.
- Maintaining appropriate professional boundaries.
- Escalating serious security or safety concerns.
- Avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private reports.

Moderators should distinguish between legitimate disagreement and actual misconduct.

Enforcement decisions should be based on relevant conduct, available evidence, severity, context, and applicable policies.

Administrative privileges must not be used to suppress lawful criticism or retaliate against good-faith reports.

## 20. Reporting Code of Conduct Violations

Participants who experience or observe suspected misconduct are encouraged to report it through appropriate channels.

For GitHub-related misconduct, participants may use:

- GitHub's built-in abuse reporting tools.
- An authorized private moderation channel, when available.
- A verified company contact channel published on the official website.
- Other applicable platform safety and reporting mechanisms.

Official corporate website:

https://grstudiosai.github.io/gr-studios-ai-about/

Official GitHub organization:

https://github.com/GRStudiosAI

GitHub abuse reporting information:

https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-acceptable-use-policies

Reports should not be submitted publicly when doing so would expose private information or create additional safety risks.

If a verified private company reporting channel is unavailable, participants should use the relevant platform's reporting mechanisms.

## 21. Information to Include in a Report

Where appropriate, a report should contain:

- A description of the suspected misconduct.
- The affected platform or community.
- Relevant usernames or account identifiers.
- Links to relevant public content.
- Relevant dates and times.
- Sanitized screenshots or supporting evidence.
- Information about previous related incidents.
- Any immediate safety concerns.
- A preferred confidential communication method, if available.

Reports should be factual and relevant.

Participants must not fabricate evidence, impersonate witnesses, or intentionally submit false accusations.

Unnecessary personal information and confidential credentials should not be included.

A report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically considered a malicious or false report.

## 22. Confidentiality of Reports

Reports of misconduct should be handled with appropriate discretion.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary for:

- Reviewing the reported conduct.
- Conducting an investigation.
- Protecting affected participants.
- Applying appropriate enforcement measures.
- Reviewing an appeal.
- Coordinating with platform administrators.
- Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance.

Individuals involved in reviewing reports should avoid unnecessary disclosure of reporter identities and sensitive evidence.

## 23. Investigation and Review Procedures

Reported violations may be evaluated through the following process:

1. Receipt of the report.
2. Preliminary assessment.
3. Review of available evidence.
4. Assessment of immediate safety or security concerns.
5. Consideration of relevant context.
6. Communication with affected parties where appropriate.
7. Determination of whether a violation occurred.
8. Selection of proportionate enforcement measures.
9. Communication of the outcome where appropriate.
10. Documentation and follow-up.
11. Appeal review when applicable.

The investigation process may vary depending on the seriousness of the reported conduct, available evidence, privacy considerations, and operational circumstances.

Emergency restrictions may be applied before an investigation is completed when reasonably necessary to protect individuals or systems.

## 24. Enforcement Framework

Enforcement measures should be proportionate to the seriousness, frequency, intent, and impact of the misconduct.

| Level | Classification | Possible Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Minor misconduct | Guidance, correction, or informal reminder |
| Level 2 | Documented misconduct | Formal warning or content removal |
| Level 3 | Repeated or significant misconduct | Temporary participation restrictions |
| Level 4 | Serious misconduct | Extended suspension or removal of privileges |
| Level 5 | Severe or persistent misconduct | Permanent exclusion from affected communities |
| Emergency | Immediate safety or security threat | Immediate protective restrictions and appropriate escalation |

Enforcement is not required to follow a strictly sequential progression.

Serious violations may justify immediate restrictions without prior warnings.

## 25. Available Moderation Actions

Depending on the circumstances, authorized administrators may:

- Issue informal guidance.
- Issue formal warnings.
- Request correction of inappropriate content.
- Remove prohibited content.
- Lock discussions or issue threads.
- Restrict commenting privileges.
- Restrict contribution privileges.
- Remove administrative permissions.
- Temporarily suspend participation.
- Permanently restrict participation.
- Report misconduct to platform administrators.
- Refer serious matters to appropriate authorities where lawful and necessary.

Enforcement actions must remain within the authority granted by the applicable platform and applicable law.

Restrictions should be limited to the affected environments unless broader action is reasonably justified.

## 26. Appeals and Reconsideration

Individuals subject to significant enforcement actions may request reconsideration through an available authorized communication channel.

An appeal should include:

- Identification of the enforcement action.
- A concise explanation of the disagreement.
- Relevant supporting information.
- Any circumstances that may have been overlooked.
- A requested resolution.

Appeals should be submitted respectfully and must not involve harassment, threats, impersonation, or repeated disruption.

Where reasonably possible, appeals should be reviewed by an authorized individual who was not directly responsible for the original decision.

Possible appeal outcomes include:

- Confirmation of the original decision.
- Modification of restrictions.
- Reduction of the enforcement period.
- Reversal of the enforcement action.
- Reopening of the investigation.

An appeal does not automatically suspend an existing restriction.

Platform-level enforcement decisions may require an appeal directly to the relevant platform provider.

## 27. Protection Against Retaliation

Retaliation against individuals who submit good-faith reports, participate in investigations, provide relevant evidence, or exercise legitimate appeal rights is prohibited.

Retaliation may include:

- Threats or intimidation.
- Targeted harassment.
- Improper removal of privileges.
- Disclosure of private information.
- Coordinated exclusion.
- Abuse of administrative authority.
- Attempts to pressure individuals into withdrawing legitimate reports.

Good-faith participation in a reporting or investigation process must not itself be treated as misconduct.

Knowingly malicious reporting may be addressed separately when supported by appropriate evidence.

## 28. Repeated Violations and Restriction Circumvention

Individuals must not deliberately circumvent legitimate participation restrictions.

Prohibited circumvention may include:

- Creating alternative accounts to evade an active restriction.
- Using another individual's account to bypass enforcement.
- Directing others to continue prohibited conduct.
- Reposting removed prohibited content for disruptive purposes.
- Misrepresenting identity to regain restricted access.

Repeated violations and deliberate circumvention may result in additional enforcement measures.

Creating or maintaining multiple accounts for legitimate purposes is not automatically a violation.

## 29. Third-Party Platforms and External Communities

GR Studios AI LLC may operate communities through independently managed third-party platforms.

These platforms may include GitHub, Discord, social media services, application distribution platforms, gaming services, and other communication providers.

Participants must comply with both this Code of Conduct and applicable third-party platform requirements.

GR Studios AI LLC does not control the independent moderation decisions, account restrictions, or enforcement procedures of third-party service providers.

Where platform rules conflict with community procedures, applicable platform requirements and legal obligations must be respected.

## 30. Legal Rights and Policy Limitations

This Code of Conduct is intended to establish standards for participation in company-managed environments.

It is not intended to:

- Restrict lawful criticism.
- Prevent legitimate consumer complaints.
- Prohibit legally protected reporting.
- Override applicable employment protections.
- Restrict lawful whistleblowing.
- Prevent cooperation with authorized investigations.
- Override applicable intellectual property exceptions.
- Waive statutory or contractual rights.
- Replace applicable platform terms.
- Establish unrestricted authority over unrelated private activities.

Nothing in this policy eliminates rights or protections established by applicable law.

Separate contractual agreements may establish additional obligations for employees, contractors, customers, and business partners.
